✦ What Sojourn Can Do

Sojourn is your family's all-in-one travel companion — built to help you plan before the trip, discover during it, and remember it long after you're home.

🗺️

Plan

Build a day-by-day storyboard for your trip. Add activities, meals, travel, and must-dos — organized by date with times and categories.

📷

Discover

Drop a pin on spontaneous finds — a hidden beach, a great taco stand — with a photo and note. Builds a live map of your trip as you go.

📖

Journal

Write a few lines each day. Sojourn prompts you with a question to spark the memory and saves each entry tied to that exact trip day.

✦

AI Planner

Ask the built-in AI assistant things like "Top 10 things to do in El Salvador" and tap to add suggestions directly to your itinerary.

🗺️ How to Create a Trip

  1. 1 Open Sojourn and tap the + button in the top right of the Home screen.
  2. 2 Enter your trip name (e.g. "Family Trip to El Salvador") and destination.
  3. 3 Choose an emoji icon that represents your trip.
  4. 4 Set your start and end dates.
  5. 5 Tap Add — your trip appears on the Home screen and is ready to plan.

📋 How to Plan Your Itinerary

Adding activities manually

  1. 1 Tap your trip from the Home screen to open it.
  2. 2 You'll land on the Plan tab. Tap a day in the strip at the top to select it.
  3. 3 Tap the + button in the top right, or tap Add manually on the empty state screen.
  4. 4 Enter a title, optional details, and pick a category (Food, Activity, Travel, Stay, Must-do, or Note).
  5. 5 Optionally set a time for the activity and tap Add.

Using the AI Planner

  1. 1 On the Plan tab, tap the ✦ AI button in the top right toolbar.
  2. 2 Type a prompt like "Top 10 things to do in El Salvador" or tap one of the quick idea chips.
  3. 3 The AI returns up to 8 suggestions with details and categories.
  4. 4 Tap the + icon on any suggestion to add it directly to that day's timeline. Tap Add all to add everything at once.

Editing or completing an activity

  1. 1 Tap any activity on the timeline to open the edit sheet.
  2. 2 Update the title, details, category, or time and tap Save.
  3. 3 To mark it done, tap the circle checkbox on the right side of the activity row.
  4. 4 To delete it, open the edit sheet and tap Delete activity at the bottom.

📍 How to Use Discover

Adding a spot

  1. 1 Open your trip and tap the Discover tab.
  2. 2 Tap the + button or the dashed Add spot card in the grid.
  3. 3 Tap the photo area to pick an image from your library.
  4. 4 Enter a place name and a short caption about the experience.
  5. 5 Toggle Use my current location on to auto-pin your GPS coordinates.
  6. 6 Tap Add — the spot appears in your photo grid and on the map.

Sharing a spot to Instagram

  1. 1 Tap a spot card to open the detail view.
  2. 2 Tap the camera icon in the top right toolbar.
  3. 3 If Instagram is installed, it opens Instagram Stories with your photo pre-loaded. If not, the iOS share sheet opens instead.

✍️ How to Use the Journal

Writing an entry

  1. 1 Open your trip and tap the Journal tab.
  2. 2 Tap the Write today's entry card at the top, or tap the pencil icon in the toolbar.
  3. 3 Give your entry a title and pick a mood (Excited, Happy, Funny, etc.).
  4. 4 Write freely in the body area. Tap a writing prompt chip at the bottom if you need a nudge.
  5. 5 Tap Save — the entry is stored tied to that day of your trip.

Reading or editing a past entry

  1. 1 Tap any entry card in the Journal tab to open it in full.
  2. 2 Tap the pencil icon in the top right to edit the title, body, or mood.
  3. 3 To delete, scroll to the bottom of the edit sheet and tap Delete entry.

🔗 Sharing and Deleting Trips

Sharing a trip

  1. 1 On the Home screen, swipe left on any trip card.
  2. 2 Tap the teal Share button.
  3. 3 The iOS share sheet opens with the message: "I planned a trip for us to [destination]! Check it out on the Sojourn app on the App Store."
  4. 4 Choose Messages, Mail, AirDrop, or any app to share.

Deleting a trip

  1. 1 On the Home screen, swipe left on the trip card you want to remove.
  2. 2 Tap the red Delete button.
  3. 3 A confirmation dialog appears — tap Delete trip to confirm. This permanently removes the trip and all its plans, spots, and journal entries.

💬 Frequently Asked Questions

Will my trips sync across multiple devices?
Currently, Sojourn stores all data locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. iCloud sync across devices is planned for a future update.
Does the AI Planner require an internet connection?
Yes — the AI Planner sends your prompt to Claude's API to generate suggestions, so an active internet connection is required for that feature. All other app features (planning, journaling, discover) work fully offline.
How do I change the app to dark mode?
Tap the appearance icon in the top left of the Home screen. It cycles through three modes: System (follows your iPhone settings), Light, and Dark. Your preference is saved and remembered across launches.
Can I add photos to journal entries?
Photo attachments for journal entries are coming in a future update. Right now, photos are supported in the Discover tab when logging a spot.
I deleted a trip by accident — can I recover it?
Unfortunately, deleted trips cannot be recovered at this time. We recommend using the confirmation dialog carefully before deleting. Trip backup and restore is a planned future feature.
Why does the AI Planner say it can't connect?
This usually means there's no internet connection, or the AI service is temporarily unavailable. Check your connection and try again. If the problem persists, please report it using the bug form below.
How do I request access to or delete my account data?
You can submit a data request at any time using the link in our Privacy Policy, or email us directly at dakota.lowery89@icloud.com.
